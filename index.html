life-tools

life-tools is a set of personal record-keeping tools (Discord bots) used only by their owner. They write the owner's own records into the owner's own Google Sheets and Google Drive.

life-tools 是個人用的記錄工具（Discord bot），只有擁有者自己使用，把記錄寫進擁有者自己的 Google 試算表和雲端硬碟。

The app is not offered to the public. / 不對外提供服務。

Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策
