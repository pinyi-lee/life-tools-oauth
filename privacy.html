Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

  • life-tools is used only by its owner. No one else signs in to it.
  • It uses the Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs to write and read the owner's own records in the owner's own spreadsheets and folders.
  • Data accessed through Google APIs is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account > Third-party connections.

隱私權政策

  • life-tools 只有擁有者自己使用，沒有其他人登入。
  • 透過 Google Sheets 和 Google Drive API，讀寫擁有者自己的試算表和資料夾裡的記錄。
  • 透過 Google API 取得的資料不出售、不分享或轉交給任何第三方，也不用於廣告。
  • 隨時可以在 Google 帳戶的「第三方連結」撤銷存取權。
